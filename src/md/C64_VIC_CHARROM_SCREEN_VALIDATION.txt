C64 VIC-II character-ROM screen validation
============================================

- Screen matrix: $8C00-$8FE7
- Owner table:   $8800-$8BE7
- Bitmap:        $A000-$BF3F
- D018:          $38
- VIC character-ROM window $9000-$9FFF is not used as screen matrix.
- Owner and screen tails clear 232 bytes with X=$00..$E7.
